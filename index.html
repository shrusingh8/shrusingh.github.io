Research

Focus Areas

AI in Healthcare & Medical Education

I investigate how AI models can enhance diagnostic decision-making and student assessment. My research showed that LLM performance on medical benchmarks may be inflated, with a 39.43% performance deterioration when shifting from multiple-choice to free-response questions.

Genomics & Cellular Biology

I use advanced nano-sequencing technologies and high-throughput screening to understand genomic mutations, cellular development, and mechanisms of long-term genomic stability.

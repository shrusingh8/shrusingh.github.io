Focus Areas
AI in Healthcare & Medical Education

Rethinking how medical AI is evaluated

I investigate how AI models can enhance diagnostic decision-making and student assessment. My research revealed that LLM performance on medical benchmarks may be significantly inflated, with a 39.43% performance deterioration when shifting from multiple-choice to free-response questions.

Genomics & Cellular Biology

Understanding mutations and cellular mechanisms

I use advanced nano-sequencing technologies and high-throughput screening to understand genomic mutations, cellular development, and long-term genomic stability.
